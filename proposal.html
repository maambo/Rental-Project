Overview

Executive summary

This proposal outlines the full scope of the Rental Property Management Platform — a web-based system designed to digitise and streamline every aspect of the landlord-tenant relationship in Zambia. The platform replaces manual processes with automated, traceable, and mobile-friendly workflows: from listing properties and screening tenants, to collecting rent and handling disputes.

The platform has been designed with the Zambian context in mind — supporting local mobile money providers (Airtel, MTN, Zamtel), the Zambia location hierarchy (Province → District → Town), and local ID verification (NRC). It is built on proven, enterprise-grade technology that scales from a single landlord to a nationwide property agency.

Scope of work

Proposed modules

The following 13 modules extend the core platform with advanced capabilities. Each is a self-contained deliverable with its own database, backend logic, and user interface.

1
Landlord Subscription & Membership Tiers
In progress

A tiered subscription system that gates the number of properties a landlord can list. Free Starter accounts can list 1 property; paid tiers unlock 5, 15, or unlimited listings. The platform automatically enforces limits and guides landlords to upgrade.

  • Starter — Free — 1 property listing
  • Basic — K99/month — 5 properties
  • Professional — K299/month — 15 properties + reports
  • Enterprise — K699/month — unlimited + priority support
2
Tenant Premium Membership
Planned

A premium tier for tenants that makes their applications stand out to landlords. Priority Applicant badge, saved application templates, downloadable reference letters, and early access to property alerts.

  • Tenant Free — basic search and applications
  • Tenant Plus — K49/month — priority badge, search alerts, reference letters
3
Mobile Money & Digital Payments
Planned

Full integration with Zambia's major mobile money providers and card payments, replacing manual proof-of-payment uploads. Tenants pay rent directly through the platform; landlords receive funds with automatic transaction reconciliation and a 1.5% platform fee.

  • Supported channels: Airtel Money, MTN MoMo, Zamtel, Visa/Mastercard
  • Webhook-based instant payment confirmation
  • Landlord payout dashboard (withdraw to bank or mobile wallet)
  • Full transaction history and receipt generation
4
Skilled Workers Marketplace
Planned

A dedicated marketplace connecting property owners and tenants with verified local tradespeople. Builders, plumbers, electricians, painters, welders, and more can register their services, set their own rates, and receive bookings through the platform. The platform earns a commission on in-platform bookings.

  • Trade categories: building, plumbing, electrical, painting, welding, roofing, and more
  • Worker profiles with portfolio photos, trade certificates, and KYC verification
  • Booking calendar, quote system, and in-app messaging
  • Rating and review system (verified completed jobs only)
  • Worker tiers: Free, Verified (K150 one-time), Standard (K79/month), Premium (K149/month)
5
Smart Notification System
Planned

Real-time alerts across email, SMS, and in-app notifications for every important event on the platform. No user needs to keep checking the platform — the platform comes to them.

  • Application status updates, new messages, lease signings, payment confirmations
  • Rent due reminders for tenants, payment received alerts for landlords
  • Subscription expiry warnings, new job booking notifications for workers
  • In-app notification bell with unread count
6
Property Search Alerts
Planned

Tenants save their search criteria (location, price range, bedrooms, property type) and receive automatic notifications whenever a new matching property is approved and listed. No more manually refreshing the search page.

  • Multiple saved searches per tenant
  • Daily alert digest or instant notification on new listing
  • Available to Tenant Plus subscribers
7
Financial Reports & Analytics
Planned

Landlords can view income summaries across their entire portfolio — broken down by property, month, and tenant. Downloadable CSV and PDF statements for tax purposes and personal records.

  • Monthly and annual income summaries
  • Rent collection rate per property
  • CSV export for accounting software
  • Available to Professional and Enterprise tier landlords
8
Move-In / Move-Out Inspection Reports
Planned

Landlords create a room-by-room inspection checklist with photos before a tenant moves in. At move-out, a second inspection is completed and compared against the original. Both parties acknowledge the reports digitally — creating a clear, admissible record for deposit disputes.

  • Photo documentation per room and item
  • Condition rating: Excellent / Good / Fair / Poor
  • Digital acknowledgement by both landlord and tenant
  • Before/after comparison view
9
Dispute Resolution Centre
Planned

A formal mediation channel for landlord-tenant conflicts — deposit deductions, maintenance failures, lease breaches, and more. Both parties upload evidence; an admin mediator reviews and posts a binding resolution. Everything is documented and timestamped.

  • Dispute types: deposit, maintenance, lease breach, other
  • Evidence upload (photos, documents, receipts)
  • Admin mediation panel with resolution notes
  • Both parties notified of outcome via email and SMS
10
Public Landlord Profiles
Planned

Every landlord gets a public profile page accessible without login — showing their verified status, star rating, active listings, total properties managed, and tenant reviews. Builds trust and drives organic discovery via search engines.

  • Verified badge tied to subscription tier
  • Average rating from verified tenant reviews
  • Response rate and average response time
  • Active property listings on the profile
11
Referral & Rewards Program
Planned

Every user gets a unique referral link. When someone they refer joins and completes their first transaction, the referrer earns K20 platform credit — redeemable against their next subscription payment. Drives organic growth at low cost.

  • Unique referral link per user
  • Reward triggered on referred user's first payment
  • K20 platform credit per successful referral
  • Referral dashboard to track invites and credits earned
12
Tenant Identity & Document Verification
Planned

Tenants upload and verify their identity and financial documents — NRC, employment letters, payslips, and bank statements. Landlords see a "Verified Tenant" badge on applications, reducing the risk of bad tenants and speeding up the approval process.

  • Supported documents: NRC, employment letter, payslip, bank statement
  • Admin verification review with approve/reject
  • "Verified Tenant" badge visible on all applications
M
Mobile Android Application
Planned

A native Android app giving users access to all platform features on the go. The backend API is already in place — this module delivers the mobile client: property browsing, applications, rent payments, messaging, and push notifications.

  • Property search and browsing with map
  • Tenant applications and payment via Airtel/MTN/card
  • Real-time chat and push notifications
  • Landlord dashboard — view applications, manage properties
  • Worker search and booking

Delivery

Implementation timeline

Delivery is structured in four phases, each producing a working, testable product increment. The client receives access to a staging environment at the end of each phase.

Phase 1 — Weeks 1–3
Subscription system (complete) + core platform handover
Core platform delivered for client review. Subscription enforcement, landlord tiers, and admin subscription management completed. Client signs off on existing functionality.
Phase 2 — Weeks 4–8
Digital payments & banking integration
Full Airtel/MTN/Zamtel/card payment integration via PayChangu gateway. Tenant rent payments, landlord payout dashboard, admin transaction ledger, and automatic reconciliation.
Phase 3 — Weeks 9–16
Skilled workers marketplace
Worker profiles, trade categories, service listings, booking flow, payment-on-completion with commission deduction, worker KYC verification, and public browse/search pages.
Phase 4 — Weeks 17–22
Notifications, reporting, inspections, disputes & remaining modules
SMS/email notification system, financial reports and CSV export, move-in/out inspection reports, dispute resolution centre, public landlord profiles, tenant document verification, referral system, and saved search alerts.
Phase 5 — Weeks 23–30
Mobile Android application
Native Android app development consuming the existing API. Property browsing, applications, payments, messaging, push notifications, worker search. Submitted to Google Play Store.
